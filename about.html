History

GRABIRON RAY Attachments

At the beginning of the 1990s, our plant produced equipment and machinery for mining and metallurgical companies.

In 2006, we became subcontractors for Finnish companies manufacturing mining equipment.

After 9 years of high-quality production, we founded Grabiron LLC jointly with the British company Corrosioneering Group Ltd, while also manufacturing steel structures for excavator attachments for Atlas Copco.

Quality Standards

For 12 years, our plant has exported products for Sandvik and Metso; recently we also manufacture key parts for Atlas Copco attachments and apply their production standards in our own products.

With ISO 9001 certification, deep manufacturing experience, and hydraulics from leading European suppliers, our products are not only on par with European analogs, but in many cases exceed them.

We have a full production cycle, including casting and assembly, and purchase only standard components from Italian and Swedish manufacturers.

Partnership and Development

Since 2025, we have signed an exclusive agreement with the popular Chinese attachment manufacturer Ray Attachments, significantly expanding the product range sold in Ukraine.

In recent years, China’s machine-building industry has made a technological leap, and Ray Attachments has proven in its segment that European quality at a competitive price is the new reality.
